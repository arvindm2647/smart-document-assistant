Working Hours Policy - Acme Corporation

1. Standard Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour
lunch break. The company operates on a 40-hour work week.

2. Flexible Working
Employees may request flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, subject to
manager approval, as long as core hours (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM) are maintained.

3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require
approval from both the reporting manager and HR.

4. Overtime
Non-exempt employees who work beyond 40 hours in a week are eligible for overtime pay at
1.5 times their regular hourly rate, subject to prior manager approval.

Leave Policy - Acme Corporation

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused annual
leave of up to 5 days may be carried forward to the following year with manager approval.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

3. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave and 2 weeks of paid paternity
leave, in accordance with local labor law.

4. Leave Application Process
Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 3 working days in
advance for planned leave. Emergency leave should be reported to the reporting manager
as soon as possible.

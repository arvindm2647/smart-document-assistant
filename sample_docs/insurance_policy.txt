Employee Insurance Policy - Acme Corporation

1. Health Insurance
All employees are covered under the group health insurance plan from their first day of
employment. Coverage includes hospitalization, outpatient treatment up to INR 25,000 per
year, and dental care up to INR 10,000 per year.

2. Dependent Coverage
Employees may add up to 4 dependents (spouse, children, and parents) to the health
insurance plan. Dependent coverage requires a one-time enrollment during the employee's
first 30 days or during the annual open enrollment window.

3. Life Insurance
The company provides a group term life insurance policy equal to 3 times the employee's
annual base salary, at no cost to the employee.

4. Claims Process
Insurance claims can be filed through the insurance provider's portal. Reimbursement
claims are typically processed within 15 business days.
